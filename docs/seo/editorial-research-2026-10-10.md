## Research and limitations

- HYPD Search Console: `sc-domain:moralclean.com`, web queries, 9 September–7 October 2026, finalized data, 100-row limit. Returned ten query rows with limited impressions. Relevant examples included steam cleaner (6 impressions, average position 38.83), steam cleaner pakistan (2, 27.5), steam cleaner karachi (2, 11), and commercial cleaning repairs (2, 4.5). These sparse observations are topic signals, not proof of sustained rankings or an exhaustive traffic total. Google withholds anonymized queries.
- Serpstat: discovered `g_pk` through its search-engine list and requested eight terms via `get_keywords_info`. Returned steam cleaner (regional volume 720, difficulty 10) and steam cleaner pakistan (390, difficulty 10). These are tool estimates, not Moral Clean traffic. The other six terms had no returned metric; this does not establish zero demand.
- Semrush: keyword report `phrase_these` in the `pk` database was rejected with `ERROR 122 :: WRONG FORMAT OR EMPTY KEY`. No Semrush volume or difficulty data was used or fabricated. A Pro subscription does not establish that the connector's API request succeeds.
- Accessory and recovery topics were selected for business relevance to the existing catalogue and service enquiries, not claimed measured demand for those exact phrases.

FAQs are visible native disclosures; no FAQ rich-result eligibility is claimed.
