## Technical and enquiry changes

- Shared metadata removes an existing repeated trailing brand suffix. Canonicals remain specific to each public route and valid pagination. Filtered/search variants remain noindex.
- All 36 product pages now expose description, full recorded specifications and service sections in HTML rather than leaving specifications inside an inactive tab.
- Product JSON-LD uses real names, descriptions, images, brands, model codes and recorded properties. No fabricated offers, prices, stock promises, ratings or reviews were added. Basic Product markup alone does **not** qualify these quote-only pages for Google's product rich results; Google's requirements include an offer, review or aggregate rating. [Google product requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-snippet)
- The empty chemicals category remains accessible, receives noindex and is excluded from the sitemap and main public category navigation.
- Filter options are scoped to the selected category; unassociated tags are omitted from public facets. This reduces irrelevant combinations, including orphan test tags.
- Invalid pagination previously triggered Supabase's `PGRST103` range error and a 500. Product and blog queries now recover the real filtered count; metadata validation allows the route to return 404 before rendering. Valid product pagination remains indexable with its own canonical.
- Steam and category quotation links prefill the real category and the details needed for an equipment quote.
- Public service descriptions now state assessment, compatibility and quotation requirements. Unsupported blanket longevity and turnaround claims were removed from those database records.
- Contact success and shared service-call banners use the configured business telephone. Hard-coded response-time promises were removed from enquiry forms.
- Analytics distinguish saved enquiries (`inquiry_submitted`) from phone/WhatsApp clicks and quote-request clicks. Spam honeypot responses and failed database inserts do not record a lead. Notification failure does not hide a saved enquiry. Form values are not passed to analytics; autocapture and session recording are disabled.

Database changes were limited to adding the steam category and updating three service records. The original public records are backed up in `backups/catalog-before-2026-10-09.json`. Existing local homepage/favicon edits were preserved and included in the website deployment. No product records were removed or redirected to unrelated products.
