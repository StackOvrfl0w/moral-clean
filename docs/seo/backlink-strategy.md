# Moral Clean: 90-day backlink strategy

Earn relevant referrals from manufacturers, customers and industry resources. Do not buy ranking links. No outreach has been sent and no memberships or placements have been purchased.

## Prerequisites: week 1

- Confirm the five deleted test URLs continue to return `404` after each deployment and remain absent from the sitemap. They have no equivalent replacement pages, so redirects would be misleading.
- Keep business name, address, phone and service coverage consistent in the site settings and every external profile. Confirm any authorized-distributor claims with the brand before using them in outreach.
- Deploy the technical changes, verify Search Console access and record the initial referring domains, search clicks and quote inquiries. No baseline backlink count has been established here.

## Publish assets worth citing: weeks 2–4

1. **Scrubber dryer selection worksheet for Pakistan facilities.** Include floor area, obstacles, aisle width, tank capacity, battery runtime and an honest worked example using verified model specifications. Explain theoretical versus practical cleaning capacity. Link to `/products/category/walk-behind-scrubber` and `/products/category/ride-on-scrubber-dryer`.
2. **Cleaning machine repair-versus-replacement worksheet.** Document downtime, labor, parts availability and repair history. Use supplier quotes or label all figures as examples; do not invent savings. Link to `/services` and `/contact`.
3. **One documented customer case study.** Record the starting problem, machine/model, intervention and measured result. Obtain permission to use the customer name, images and figures. Include the measurement period and limitations.

Publish useful HTML pages first. A downloadable worksheet can accompany a page, but the explanation and source data should remain readable on the site. Drafts are not backlink destinations.

## Weekly execution: weeks 5–12

The owner or sales lead identifies 5 relevant prospects per week, records the connection and approves the factual pitch. The content owner supplies a specific resource relevant to each prospect. Send at most one individualized follow-up after 7–10 business days, then stop unless the prospect responds. Sending outreach requires a separate instruction; this document does not authorize it.

For each opportunity, record: organization, source URL, relationship, public contact channel, proposed target URL, reason it helps their audience, evidence available, owner, outreach date, response, live link URL, link attributes, referral visits and qualified inquiries. Use a shared tracker with one row per opportunity; do not purchase email lists.

## Draft resource pitch

Subject: [Specific resource] for your [facility/procurement] readers

Your page [exact page] discusses [specific problem]. We have published [resource], including [original evidence or practical tool]. It may help readers [specific use]. If it is relevant, the source is [canonical URL]. [Name and role]

Replace every bracket with verified details. Do not imply prior contact, customer results or manufacturer authorization that cannot be substantiated.

## Measurement and boundaries

- Weekly: track researched prospects, relevant responses, earned referring domains, working destination URLs and referral inquiries.
- Monthly: review Search Console clicks/impressions for the linked pages and branded searches; record qualified quote inquiries and assisted conversions where available.
- At day 90: compare with the week-1 baseline. A planning target is 3–6 relevant earned referring domains and 2 publishable customer/manufacturer collaborations, not a promised result. Revise based on response and lead quality.
- Avoid automated link blasts, private blog networks, paid dofollow placements, unrelated directories, keyword-stuffed anchor text and compulsory reciprocal links. Sponsorships must be clearly disclosed and links qualified as `sponsored` or `nofollow`. [Google link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam)
