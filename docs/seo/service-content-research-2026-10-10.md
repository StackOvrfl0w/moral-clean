# Service content research and scope — 10 October 2026

## Why these pages

The service hub combined motor repair, machine parts and brush refilling into one page. These are distinct enquiries with different identification and assessment requirements. Added three pages for those existing services rather than producing overlapping buying guides.

HYPD Search Console returned finalized web page data for 10 July–7 October 2026 (16 rows, not truncated). The service hub had 4 impressions, 0 clicks and average position 7.5. That small sample supports existing exposure, but does not establish meaningful search demand, reliable ranking or today's indexing status. Preview-only guides cannot yet demonstrate production search performance.

Serpstat's Pakistan Google database (`g_pk`) returned estimates for two of six requested terms: “mop trolley” 90 monthly searches and “floor polishing machine” 70. It returned no metrics for the repair phrases. These service pages therefore address an observed content and enquiry gap; their traffic potential is not validated by a keyword-volume figure. No difficulty value was returned for those two terms.

Semrush supplied no usable keyword results in the preceding work because its connector rejected the API key/format. A paid subscription is not evidence that this connector has working API access. No Semrush metrics are attributed to this release.

Google's [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) informed the emphasis on practical details and trustworthy claims. Content volume alone is not a ranking strategy.

## Implementation

- `/services/cleaning-machine-repair`: machine identification, symptom reporting, repair scope, repair-versus-replacement assessment.
- `/services/cleaning-machine-parts`: model and component identification, compatibility, supply-versus-fitting scope, availability confirmation.
- `/services/brush-refilling`: brush body and fitting condition, floor application, refurbishment-versus-replacement assessment.
- About 1,436 words across introductions, sections and FAQs, plus preparation checklists and related links. No invented stock levels, prices, qualifications, turnaround guarantees or service coverage.
- Existing equipment-service photographs, navy/cyan layout, responsive enquiry sidebar and native FAQ disclosures.
- Unique titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, social images, Service and BreadcrumbList structured data. No claim that schema guarantees a rich result.
- Hub links, two contextual guide links, cross-service links and sitemap inclusion.
- Removed unsupported same-day response, automatic original-parts and nationwide field-service promises from the hub. Location, parts and terms require job-specific confirmation.

No database writes, connector settings changes, production promotion or master push are part of this release. Existing contact form service values are retained, including legacy slug spellings.
